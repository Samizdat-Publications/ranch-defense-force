NOBODY RUNS BY ANY MORE

Three people mulling, and the hazmat suit was walking backwards

Two separate bugs, both yours to have caught. The hazmat suit really was moving backwards — the wrapper translated left while the layer played walk.right. And every leg ended by cutting straight to a front-facing pose, because the idle layer was walk.down frame 0 and there is no turn art between a profile and a front view.

The fly-throughs are gone. Nobody crosses the room. Each figure mulls: idle in profile → a short leg → idle in profile facing the way they went. Tech 220px on 47s, guard 320px on 59s, hazmat 300px on 41s — all at 120px/s against a 1.2s eight-frame cycle, so the stride covers 144px and the feet don't skate.
Direction now always matches the strip. Leftward legs play walk.left, rightward play walk.right. That is the whole of the backwards fix.
Nobody faces front. The idle pose is frame 0 of the profile walk, held. A figure turns by standing still in the new profile for ~0.8s before setting off, which reads as deciding rather than snapping — and it needs no art that doesn't exist. When you generate turn frames, they drop into exactly that 0.8s window.

Lanes still matter for paint order: hazmat at foot 770 is pushed into the DOM before the console, jarRack, drumRank and the near band, so all of them occlude him. Tech 870, guard 960. The rule the yard's groundActor already encodes — z-index comes from footY — is what a hand-placed DOM order is standing in for here.
