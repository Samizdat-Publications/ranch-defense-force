RANCH DEFENSE FORCE — DESIGN PASS 1 OF 4

The Card System

Upgrade cards are the main thing the player touches. This pass replaces the carved-wood panel language with Paper & Pin: every card is a printed seed packet, and rarity is a colour-coded tin plate riveted across it. Five named tiers, a redundant non-colour channel, and a deal animation. Content is placeholder — the full roster lands in pass 3.

01 / ANATOMY

The seed packet

One silhouette for every card in the game — level-up, shop, Homestead, class select. A kraft packet with a punched hang tab, a window cut for the art, and a perforation across the middle.

AHang tab & punch hole. Reads as a real packet, and gives the deck a shape you recognise face-down.
BKind band. WEAPON / FIELD KIT / ROUNDS / MINION. Sorts the draw at a glance.
CArt window. Recessed dark field, 32px sprite at 4× — integer zoom, never 2.5×.
DRarity plate. Stamped tin riveted over the window's lower edge. Tier named, flanked by 1–5 pips.
EName in wood type. Two lines maximum. The one place the game gets to be loud.
FPerforation. Divides the sell from the numbers.
GStat block. Signed deltas, mono, right-aligned. Green is better, red is the cost.
HFooter. Lot number on a level-up, feed price in the shop, tier repeated in small caps.

02 / RARITY

Five badges

Rarity is a stamped tin plate riveted across the bottom of the art window — the standard colour ladder, grey, green, blue, red, orange, with the tier named on the plate. It sits in the same place on every card, so the eye learns one spot.

The redundant non-colour channels are the word itself and the pip count flanking it — one to five. Greyscale the row and all five still separate. Legendary adds a foil sweep across the plate; nothing else animates, so the sweep means one thing.

03 / LEVEL-UP, IN SITU

Dealt from the deck

Cards arc up out of a deck at the bottom of the screen, 110ms apart, and land with a small overshoot. The rarity plate stamps down after the card settles — a separate beat, so the tier is the last thing you read. A Legendary sits a little higher than its neighbours and gold keeps sweeping across the plate.

04 / THE SHOP

Same packet, priced

The shop is the same card with two additions: a feed price in the footer, and a bulldog clip when you lock a card for the next visit. Shop-exclusive run-definers need no overprint — the price is the tell, and the roster in pass 3 will make the split by tier. Unaffordable cards go to pulpboard grey and the plate loses its emboss and drop shadow — they read as unprinted stock, not as disabled UI.

05 / HANDOFF

What to change

rarity.json
Replace colour, drop glow and badge. Add dark (the plate's lower stop), ink (plate text), rank (pip count) and foil. Weights and luckScaling unchanged.
MOTION
Deal 560ms cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), stagger 110ms. The plate stamps down at +340ms, 380ms with a 1.55× overshoot and a 0.8° rest tilt. Legendary foil 2.6s loop. All CSS keyframes — no library, no per-frame canvas work.
ART WINDOW
One 32px sprite at 4× = 128px, centred. Guns are 20×6 in the pack; they take 6× and sit on the window's horizon line. Never a fractional zoom.
ACCESSIBILITY
The tier is spelled out on the plate and flanked by 1–5 pips, so colour is never the only channel. Epic ships red per the standard ladder; a violet option is a tweak away if red reads too close to the damage red already in the HUD.
