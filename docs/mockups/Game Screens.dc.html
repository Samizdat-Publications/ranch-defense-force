In play, and between

The four remaining screens in the Paper & Pin language: the HUD you read while something is trying to kill you, the pause you opened by accident, the results you actually care about, and the Homestead at dusk. Same rules as the cards — kraft paper, stamped tin, wood type, and nothing in the centre of the field.

01 / HUD · WAVE 14, BOSS ON THE FIELD

Everything hugs the edge

The centre stays clear, so every element is pinned to a rail: health and armour top-left, the wave clock top-centre with the boss bar under it, feed top-right, the weapon ring along the bottom with the ability plate beside it, and the XP strip across the very bottom edge. Nothing floats.

THE HP CHASER, KEPT
The white band draining behind the red is the best thing in the current HUD and it survives untouched — same delayed CSS transition, now on a cream paper bar instead of a grey one. A big hit still reads as a big hit.
ARMOUR AS PIPS
Armour is five diamonds under the bar, filled to your rank, rather than a number nobody converts. It uses the same diamond as the rarity plate — the only glyph in the game, now meaning "one step" everywhere.
COOLDOWN AS A WIPE
Weapon tags keep the existing scaleY shade — cheap, no layout, already wired in hud.ts. Tier is a small stamped chip rather than a bare digit, so a T4 weapon looks like it was upgraded.
CENTRE 70% CLEAR
Nothing sits inside the play area. The wave clock stays above the first crop line, the boss bar is pinned under it at the top rather than floating over the boss, and the tallest bottom element is 96px, leaving the field's lower third readable.

WHAT THIS REPLACES
All of the panel language in style.css — the carved-wood border-image 9-slice, wood-as-chrome, and gold-means-affordable. Said explicitly, as the brief asks: do not preserve it. Paper & Pin replaces it everywhere, and the fill keyword hazard disappears with the border-image itself.
WHAT IS KEPT
Three things earned their place and stay: the HP chaser, the scaleY cooldown wipe, and affordable-gets-an-outline on every purchase surface. Also kept: pause owning the audio sliders instead of a settings screen of its own.
MOTION BUDGET
Every animation here is CSS keyframes on transform, opacity or width — no library, no per-frame canvas work, nothing added to the hot loop. The HUD keeps writing to the DOM only when a value actually changes, exactly as it does today.
STILL OPEN
Two of the four Homestead signs use borrowed art — the Seed Catalog and the County Fair want their own icons. Enemies on the HUD are recoloured livestock standing in for real infected sprites. The shop is the level-up frame with a price plate, so it is not drawn again here.
