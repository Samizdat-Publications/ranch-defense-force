I was wrong about the picket blocker, and you spotted it before I did. ranch.fenceRun is drawn receding — a tall near post with panels shrinking away to the right and grass at the base. Tiling that horizontally repeats the vanishing point every 108px, which is precisely why the fence "made no sense". Enlarged side by side it is unmistakable, and the sprites file says so in its own note; I read "ranch.fenceRun stays the horizontal answer" as meaning a horizontal band, when it means the horizontal edge of a pen seen receding. The blocker is re-opened: there is no horizontal fence band in the generated set, so the near fence is LimeZu's picket tile again and the credit stays. The audit is 7 of 8, not 8 of 8. What would actually close it is one wide shallow generation asked for as a thing — "a long low wooden rail fence filling the frame edge to edge", on a 400×64 canvas.

Only the treeline is still borrowed. It wants the two unpacked oaks from the harvest-node set — name me the files and it is a five-minute swap. Sizes are scene-scale.json's drawAt, and the size is the variety: 46 down to 30 in one huddle, so the flock reads as a flock before you can see a single feather. They are gathered on ranch.feedPan, with ranch.nestBox and two ranch.eggClutch — drawn 40 wide rather than the table's 26, because a clutch you cannot see is not worth placing.

Only brahmaHen has a peck clip. The other five breeds have walk in two directions and nothing else, so a standing bird is frame 0 of its walk, held. buffHen and bantamHen have no strips at all — that is why it is six breeds and not eight. A peck for two or three more breeds would do more for this yard than any other single generation.

MOTION — MULLING, NOT PASSING BY
Nothing crosses this yard any more either. Five actors mull — rooster 67s, barred hen 53s, polish hen 43s, cat 71s, pony 61s — each idling in place, walking one short leg, and idling again. One shared keyframe set drives all of them; the leg length is a --leg custom property and the return leg is the same strip flipped on scaleX, which is the trick the yard's old rooster already used.
Joy still does her 150px errand on 43s at x 1310, foot 812 — on the barn track, in the legibility box, out of both dead zones. Third time is the last time.
The bed is unchanged: windmill.spin 4.5s, scarecrow.sway 7.4s, wheat.sway ×4 at 5.1 / 6.3 / 8.1 / 9.7s, two pecking hens, the grazing pony. One rare event: the flock crossing the sky on 29s.

Walk speeds are matched to the strips so nobody skates: hens 22px/s against a 0.8s nine-frame cycle, pony 46px/s against 1.4s. At 36.6px per metre that is 0.6 m/s for a hen and 1.25 m/s for a pony.
